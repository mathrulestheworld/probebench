Set up Probe Bench

Probe Bench runs open-weight language models on the GPU cluster of Georgia Tech's School of Industrial and Systems Engineering (ISyE), and opens them in your browser so you can generate text and look inside them. Setting up takes a few minutes of your time, then about 10 to 30 minutes of waiting while the cluster prepares the software. You only do this once.

Your ISyE UNIX account

The ISyE cluster needs its own account, called an ISyE UNIX account. It uses your regular Georgia Tech username and password, but it has to be requested separately, and someone at ISyE Computing has to turn on cluster access for it.

Don't have one yet?
  1. Request it with the ISyE UNIX account form. Off campus, you may need to be on the Georgia Tech VPN to open the form.
  2. It usually takes one or two days. You'll get an email when it's ready.
  3. Email Kelley Ross at ISyE Computing (kelley.ross@isye.gatech.edu) your username and ask for GPU cluster access.

Not started

    Setup is done

    Probe Bench is ready. Before your first session, download a model to the cluster. Models are stored there, not on your Mac, and every session can use them. Gemma 4 12B, Google's 12-billion-parameter model, is a good first choice. It's about 22 GB and downloads in a job that doesn't use a GPU, typically in 5 to 15 minutes.

    Start a GPU session

    A session reserves one GPU on the cluster and opens Probe Bench in your browser, where you load a model, generate text, and inspect activations and weights. HTCondor, the cluster's job scheduler, gives you the first free GPU that's big enough. If they're all busy, you wait in line.

      Account

      ISyE's two gateways do the same job. If one is down, switch to the other.

      Gated models

      Some models, such as Llama, need you to accept a license on Hugging Face first. Then create a token at huggingface.co/settings/tokens (Read access is enough) and save it here. It's stored only in your cluster account.

      Where things are
      On the cluster: Probe Bench in ~/probebench, models in ~/hf_cache, saved activations in ~/probebench/exports.
      On this Mac: the app's files in ~/Library/Application Support/ProbeBench, and SSH settings in ~/.ssh/config.
